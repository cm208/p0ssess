How refunds are paid

Polar is the merchant of record for p0ssess subscriptions. You pay Polar, and refunds go back through Polar to the payment method you used. We tell Polar to issue the refund and cancel the subscription, so you are not charged again. Tax on the refunded amount is returned with it. Refunds can take a few days to show up, depending on your bank.

First purchase: 30 days

If you buy Pro or Team for the first time, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of the purchase, with no questions asked. This applies to monthly and yearly plans.

Yearly renewal: 30 days

If a yearly subscription renews and you did not mean it to, you can ask for a full refund of that renewal within 30 days of the renewal date.

Monthly renewal: 7 days

If a monthly subscription renews and you did not mean it to, you can ask for a full refund of that renewal within 7 days of the renewal date.

What a refund does to your plan

A refund ends the subscription. Your plan drops to Free when we process it, the cloud sandbox turns off, and the account is handled as the terms describe for a lapsed subscription. If you only want to stop future charges and keep Pro, cancel instead, as below.

Cancel any time

You can cancel in the Polar customer portal at any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and Polar makes no further charge. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period.

Asking for a refund

Email support@p0ssess.me from the address on your account, or ask through the Polar customer portal if it offers that option. Tell us which payment you mean. We answer on a best-effort basis and aim to process a valid request within 5 business days.

Costs that are not ours to refund

OpenRouter charges for model calls are billed to your own OpenRouter key, under your own OpenRouter account. They are not part of what you pay us, and we cannot refund them. Contact OpenRouter about those.

Sandbox allowance

If you used part of the monthly sandbox allowance, a refund within the periods above is still a full refund. We do not take back a part of the amount for time you used.

Accounts we close for abuse

If we close your account for breaking the acceptable use rules in the terms, such as mining cryptocurrency, attacking other systems or trying to escape the sandbox, you do not get a refund for the current period. If we close it for any other reason, we refund the unused part of the current period. Nothing here stops us from refunding anyway.

Price changes

If we raise your price and you do not want the new price, you can cancel before the renewal and ask for a refund if the renewal has already happened and falls inside the periods above.

Disputes and the law

Please write to us before you open a dispute with your bank, because a refund is faster. You keep any chargeback rights your card issuer gives you. Polar may also refund a charge on its own to prevent a chargeback. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law where you live, including any right to cancel a digital purchase.

Contact

Write to support@p0ssess.me.
