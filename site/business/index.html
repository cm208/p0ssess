Questions before you buy

Short answers. Write to support@p0ssess.me for anything they leave out.

What leaves a person’s machine?

Prompts and files go only to the model a job is handed to, on that person’s own OpenRouter key. The p0ssess service never sees a prompt, a file or a file name. Team reports each paid run as its day, use case, model, route and cost. The cloud sandbox receives the files git tracks, to run them. Privacy policy

What do I see, and what do members see?

You see each member’s email or GitHub login, their share, and their spend by day, use case, model and route. You see no prompts, because we never receive them. Members see the rules and their own share and spend. A data processing agreement is available from legal@p0ssess.me.

What happens at the cap?

Paid runs stop on every seat and the status line turns red. Claude itself is untouched, so people keep working in Claude Code. When a member reaches their own share, only their paid runs stop. Raise the budget with p0ssess team budget.

How do I cancel, and what happens then?

Cancel or change plans in Polar’s customer portal. A first purchase can be refunded in full within 30 days, no questions asked. When Team ends, members drop to their own plan, which may be Free. Refund policy
