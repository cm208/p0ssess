The things Claude can’t do, for less.

Claude can’t watch a video, hear a recording or make an image. p0ssess hands those jobs to a model that can, and Claude answers from what comes back. This page shows what that costs, measured on the same files with and without it.

See pricingGo to the measurements

“Claude can already do that”

It has a workaround for each. The workaround stands in for input or output that Claude doesn’t have. p0ssess sends the actual file to a model that watches video with its sound, hears audio or makes images.

  • Video

    Claude alone
    Pulls still frames from the video with ffmpeg. It never hears the soundtrack.
    With p0ssess
    A model watches the video itself, with its sound.

  • Audio

    Claude alone
    Installs a local Whisper and transcribes with that.
    With p0ssess
    A model that hears audio gets the recording itself.

  • Images

    Claude alone
    Draws SVG when asked for a picture.
    With p0ssess
    A model that makes images makes the picture.
