How billing works

What a p0ssess price covers, who takes the payment and what happens when a plan ends.

  • Model calls bill to your own OpenRouter key, on every plan. The price here pays for the p0ssess service and doesn’t include the models.
  • Prices are in US dollars.
  • Checkout runs through Polar, the merchant of record. Polar handles sales tax and VAT, and gives you a portal to cancel or change plans.
  • When Pro ends you drop back to Free quietly. Account data is deleted 30 days later.
  • The cloud sandbox has to receive code to run it. What it does with your code
  • Refunds are set out in the refund policy.
