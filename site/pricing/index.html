What one rule is worth

Two jobs, measured with Claude Opus 5.5 in charge, three runs each way. Every answer was right with and without p0ssess. The figure is what one run cost, on average.

The unit-test runs used the free plugin, with a rule on the person’s own machine. The ticket runs used a team rule and nothing else, set once by an owner, which is what Team adds. Whether the saving covers the price depends on how much of this kind of work you do, and that is what the savings report measures on your own work. What a p0ssess price covers, who takes the payment and what happens when a plan ends.

The models bill separately

Checkout and tax

Checkout runs through Polar, the merchant of record. Polar handles sales tax and VAT, and gives you a portal to cancel or change plans. Refunds are set out in the refund policy.
