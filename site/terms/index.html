The cloud sandbox

The cloud sandbox runs an agent session on our hosts, in a sealed environment, using a copy of your git-tracked files. The data policy says exactly what we upload, what we skip and what we delete. In short, only git-tracked files go up, secrets and .p0ssess-private repositories are never sent, everything is deleted when the session ends, we do not log content, and the service runs in one US region at launch.

At launch a session has 4 GiB of memory, 2 CPUs and a 30-minute limit, and you can run one session at a time. By default the sandbox reaches package registries only. Full internet is an option you turn on.

The sandbox's calls to AI models travel through an encrypted connection back to your own machine, which adds your OpenRouter key and forwards the call. Your key does not reach our servers.

You run code in the sandbox at your own risk and are responsible for what you ask it to do. Review a sandbox's changes, which come back as a diff, before you keep them.
